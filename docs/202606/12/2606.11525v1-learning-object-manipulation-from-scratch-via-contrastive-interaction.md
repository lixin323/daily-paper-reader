## 摘要
对比强化学习（CRL）通过学习动力学的结构化表示，在多种以目标为导向的机器人任务中取得了近期的成功。然而，尽管在运动控制和简单控制领域取得了成功，CRL在交互丰富的操作任务中常常表现不佳。我们认为，这一困难的关键来源是物体为中心的交互，如接触或抓取，这些交互导致潜在动态模式发生显著变化。在这项工作中，我们将操作动力学建模为分段平滑的马尔可夫过程，并表明交互引起的模式变化会创建分段非线性的可达性结构，标准CRL能量函数难以表示和规划这种结构。基于此分析，我们引入了交互加权重采样（IWR）。IWR在交互前、交互中和交互后的阶段附近进行交互感知的重采样，鼓励学到的表示保留决定未来可达性的模式边界，以捕获多模态和分段非线性的可达性。在包括二维动态控制、机器人操作和机器人空气曲棍球等以交互为中心的环境中，IWR在样本效率和整体性能上均优于先前的CRL方法，在仿真中平均提升19.8%。最后，通过使用IWR训练的策略的仿真到现实流水线，我们展示了首个能够击球入门的真实世界目标导向机器人空气曲棍球智能体，成功率从25%提高到60%。项目页面：IWR-arxiv.github.io。

## Abstract
Contrastive Reinforcement Learning (CRL) has seen recent success in a wide variety of goal-conditioned robotics tasks by learning structured representations of the dynamics. However, despite its success in locomotion and simpler control domains, CRL often struggles in interaction-rich manipulation. We argue that a key source of this difficulty is object-centric interaction, such as contact or grasping, that induces distinct changes in the underlying dynamic modes. In this work, we formulate manipulation dynamics as a piecewise-smooth Markov process and show that interaction-induced mode changes create piecewise nonlinear reachability structures that are difficult for standard CRL energy functions to represent and plan over. Based on this analysis, we introduce Interaction-weighted Resampling (IWR). IWR performs interaction-aware resampling around phases before, during, and after interactions, encouraging the learned representation to preserve the mode boundaries that determine future reachability to capture multi-modal and piecewise nonlinear reachability. Across interaction-centric environments, including 2D dynamic control, robotic manipulation, and robot air hockey, IWR improves both sample efficiency and overall performance over prior CRL methods, with 19.8% average improvement in simulation. Finally, using a sim-to-real pipeline with policies trained by IWR, we demonstrate the first real-world goal-conditioned robot air hockey agent capable of hitting goals, improving success from 25% to 60%.
